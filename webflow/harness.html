typsettle · Webflow embed harness

The compositor tunes each line until the whole paragraph breathes at one even optical weight, then locks the tracking into place.

What "working" looks like: on load, each line of the paragraph appears with a slightly-off letter-spacing and animates — staggered top to bottom — to its settled tracking over ~0.9s. Because data-ts-target-tracking="auto" is set, denser lines end a touch looser and sparse lines a touch tighter, evening out the optical colour. Resizing the window regroups lines by the new width and re-runs the settle. Clicking Replay calls window.Typsettle.restart() to run it again. With OS "reduce motion" enabled, the text simply appears at its settled state with no animation (handled inside the core).
